About

About me

Yoga has been part of my life since I was a teenager. Through different seasons of life, it has been a constant — a practice that has helped me find balance, strength, and a deeper connection with myself.

Alongside teaching yoga, I work as a dietitian, where my passion lies in supporting people to take an active role in their health and wellbeing. I believe in a holistic approach to feeling well, where movement, nourishment, breath, and rest all have an important place.

My classes offer a balance of movement and stillness. Some are more active and dynamic, focusing on building strength, improving mobility, and connecting with the body. Others are slower and more calming, inviting you to slow down, release tension, and find a moment of calm in your day. Whatever the style, my aim is to create a welcoming space where you can reconnect with yourself and move in a way that feels right for you.

I teach in English, but I also speak French and can offer support in French whenever needed.
